Toroidal quantum dynamics

Worked answerHow should a Coulomb-coupled electron on a torus be modeled?

Answer

Use a scalar surface Hamiltonian with the thin-layer curvature term and a fixed Coulomb source a positive distance from the surface. For a centered source, separate azimuthal sectors and transform the poloidal equation to a flat measure before diagonalizing it. Ordinary three-dimensional hydrogen has a different operator and spectrum.

Spectral theory and quantum benchmarks

Worked answerHow can the same spatial operator organize heat, wave and Schrödinger evolution?

Answer

Choose a self-adjoint realization with the appropriate boundary conditions. Expand in eigenmodes. For nonnegative eigenvalues, the heat equation damps each mode exponentially, the wave equation gives oscillatory modes with a separate zero-mode limit, and Schrödinger evolution changes each mode by a unitary phase.

Worked answerWhy do eigenvalue problems recur across mathematical physics?

Answer

A common method is to separate spatial structure from time evolution. The operator and its domain fix the admissible modes. The eigenvalues set the characteristic energies, frequencies, or decay rates according to the governing evolution equation.

Worked answerHow can boundary conditions change the spectrum when the potential is fixed?

Answer

Fix the differential expression and compare its Dirichlet, Neumann and Robin domains. For the free interval, Dirichlet conditions exclude a constant mode and Neumann conditions admit it at zero energy. Robin parameters enter the endpoint equations and can shift eigenvalues below zero.

Worked answerWhy is the regular separated Robin Schrödinger operator self-adjoint?

Answer

For a real bounded potential on a finite interval, integration by parts gives the endpoint boundary form. The prescribed real Robin conditions make it vanish. Conversely, testing against domain functions with independently chosen endpoint values forces the same Robin conditions on an adjoint-domain function.

Worked answerHow do you show the Robin quadratic form is closed and bounded below?

Answer

On H¹(0,L), use the integral of |u′|² + V|u|² plus α|u(0)|² + β|u(L)|² for u′(0)=αu(0), u′(L)=−βu(L). A trace inequality bounds any negative endpoint terms by an arbitrarily small multiple of the derivative norm plus a multiple of the L² norm. After a sufficiently large shift, the form norm is equivalent to the H¹ norm.

Worked answerWhy is the Robin resolvent compact?

Answer

Choose a real point below the lower spectral bound. Coercivity bounds the shifted inverse from L² into H¹, and the embedding H¹(0,L) → L²(0,L) is compact. The resolvent identity extends compactness to the rest of the resolvent set.

Worked answerWhy is the Robin spectrum discrete and why do the modes form a basis?

Answer

Apply the compact self-adjoint spectral theorem to the shifted inverse. The inverse is injective and has dense range, yielding an orthonormal eigenbasis. Inverting its nonzero eigenvalues gives real eigenvalues of the original operator with finite multiplicity that tend to +∞.

Worked answerWhy are separated Robin eigenvalues simple?

Answer

For a fixed eigenvalue, the left boundary condition ties the derivative to the value at the endpoint. A solution with zero endpoint value would have zero derivative and vanish by uniqueness. Every eigenfunction is therefore determined up to one scalar by its initial value.

Worked answerHow do plane waves and Gaussian packets evolve for a free particle?

Answer

The plane wave obeys E=ℏ²k²/(2m) and has constant density. A normalized minimum-uncertainty Gaussian with initial position width σ and mean wave number k₀ moves at ℏk₀/m and spreads to σ√[1+(ℏt/(2mσ²))²]. In Wolfram Language use an unreserved variable such as energy for a symbolic check.

Worked benchmarkHow does inserting an inner hard wall change the spherical-cavity spectrum?

Answer

Write u=rR. At angular momentum zero, the reduced equation is a one-dimensional hard-wall problem on an interval of length a for the cavity and a−b for the shell. The shell energies are ℏ²π²n²/[2m(a−b)²]. For higher angular momentum, impose both wall conditions on the spherical Bessel solutions. The radial density |u|² differs from the density per unit volume.

Collective scattering in neutron-star mergers

Worked toy limitWhat does a scalar-mediator scattering benchmark establish?

Answer

At tree level the direct scalar propagator gives a momentum-space interaction proportional to −g²/(q²+μ²) in the static nonrelativistic limit. Its three-dimensional transform gives a Yukawa interaction −g² exp(−μr)/(4πr) in natural units. An identical-fermion amplitude also requires the exchange channel.
